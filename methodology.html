Our Methodology

How We Read the Market

Merit Market Pulse isn't a guess. It's built on a consistent process. Here's what shapes how we read conditions across every market we cover.

Cross-Asset Lens

We read crypto, forex, equities, and commodities together, because capital moves across markets, not inside silos. A shift in one asset class rarely happens in isolation.

Real Market Experience

Every read is grounded in 7+ years of hands-on market experience, not just a backtested model reacting to historical patterns.

Multi-Source Data

We aggregate live data from six leading providers (TradingView, CoinGecko, Investing.com, MetaTrader, Alpha Vantage, and Yahoo Finance) instead of relying on a single feed.

A Consistent Framework

Every read follows the same Discovery → Diligence → Advisory process, so conclusions are structured and repeatable, not ad hoc calls made in the moment.

Direct Access to Our Team

You get a real person to talk through what we're seeing and why, not just an automated report landing in your inbox.
