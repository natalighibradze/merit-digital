1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function, remember your preferences, and, where permitted, understand how visitors use the site. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies Merit Digital Advisory and our third-party providers use, and how you can control them. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

CategoryPurposeCan You Opt Out?
EssentialRequired for the website to function, such as remembering your cookie preference itself and keeping our site secure.No — these are necessary for the site to work.
FunctionalRemember choices you make, such as filters or display preferences, to improve your experience on return visits.Yes, via your cookie preferences.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Our website and client dashboard embed a live market data widget provided by TradingView. Loading this widget may allow TradingView to set cookies or use similar technologies on your device, subject to TradingView's own privacy practices. We only load this widget after you've had the opportunity to accept or decline non-essential cookies through our cookie banner.

Our contact form is processed by Formspree. Submitting the form is a direct action you take, rather than passive tracking, and doing so may involve standard web request handling by Formspree's servers as described in their own privacy policy.

4. Managing Your Preferences

When you first visit our website, a banner lets you accept all cookies or continue with essential cookies only. You can change your choice at any time using the button below, or by clearing your browser's cookies for this site, which will show the banner again on your next visit.

You can also control cookies directly through your browser settings, which let you block or delete cookies from some or all websites. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect how our website functions.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies and third-party tools we use. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
