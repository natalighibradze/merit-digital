The Merit Digital Story

Becoming a Trusted Name in Independent Market Research

Merit Digital Advisory was founded to bring real, hands-on market experience to businesses that don't have an in-house research team.

What started as one person's deep experience across crypto, forex, equities, and commodities is growing into a firm built on the same principle: clear, independent analysis you can actually act on.

The Merit Digital Timeline

From Hands-On Market Experience to an Independent Research Firm

Before 2019

Foundational Market Experience

Our founder builds hands-on experience across crypto, forex, equities, and commodities: the real market experience that Merit Digital Advisory's research is still built on today.

2019

Merit Digital Advisory Is Founded

The idea becomes a company, built to bring independent, institutional-style research directly to businesses, instead of keeping it in-house at trading desks and funds.

2021

Growing One Client at a Time

Word of mouth brings in the first wave of trading firms and family offices looking for research they can actually trust. The team stays small and hands-on, taking on new clients deliberately rather than chasing scale.

2024

Our Strongest Year Yet

Merit Digital Advisory brings on more new clients in a single year than ever before, expanding coverage across crypto, forex, equities, and commodities for a growing base of trading firms, asset managers, and fintech companies.

2026

A New Platform for Clients

A redesigned digital platform brings live market dashboards, weekly Merit Market Pulse updates, and research spanning 25K+ instruments across four asset classes together in one place.
