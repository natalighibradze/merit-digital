Crypto

What's Probable for Crypto Heading Into 2027

Each of Bitcoin's four halving events cuts new coin issuance in half, tightening the pace at which new supply reaches the market. Historically, the 12–18 months following a halving have coincided with the strongest price appreciation, though the effect compounds with, rather than replaces, broader liquidity conditions and demand from institutional allocators.
Forex
September 2026

Forex Into 2027: Rate Differentials Aren't Enough

Textbook theory says higher-yielding currencies should attract capital and appreciate. In practice, that relationship weakens once markets are already pricing in future rate cuts, when risk sentiment overrides carry trades, or when a currency's safe-haven status dominates its yield. Reading forex markets on rate differentials alone increasingly misses the bigger picture.
Equities
September 2026

Equities Into 2027: Breadth Is the Question That Matters

Small-caps tend to outperform large-caps in two conditions: early-cycle recoveries, when risk appetite returns before earnings do, and periods of falling rates, which disproportionately benefit smaller, more leveraged companies. Telling a genuine rotation apart from a short-lived rally usually comes down to breadth: whether the move is concentrated in a few names or spread across the index.
